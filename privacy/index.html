Privacy policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

The short version

Chart Pattern Trainer is a free educational chart-pattern reading game for Android. It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no tracking. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your score, streak, recent calls, and chosen deck are saved only on your device, in the app’s local storage (localStorage). That progress never leaves your device, and we do not receive it.

To delete it, clear the app’s data in your device settings, or uninstall the app. Either step removes the progress stored on that device.

What other services see

Like any website, the app is delivered by a hosting provider (Vercel), which keeps standard server logs such as IP address and browser type for security and reliability. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it serves them. We do not receive or use any of this data.

Practice charts

Charts in the app are generated for practice. Nothing about you is added to them, and they are not sent anywhere with your progress.

Age requirement

Chart Pattern Trainer is for people aged 13 and older. You must be 13 or older to use it. The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Educational, not financial advice

Educational, not financial advice. This trains pattern reading, not live trading. Chart Pattern Trainer is an educational drill. It does not offer trading, brokerage, or investment advice, and a correct call here is no reason to trade real money.
